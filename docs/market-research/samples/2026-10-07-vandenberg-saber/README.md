## Limits these documents state (and a buyer should hear)

- **Rule of Two: Not determined.** Mindy found 14 capable small-business parent companies among the 15 listed firms, but the supplier evidence is a sample (50 of 2,442 registered firms scored) and is statewide, not specific to 30 CONS.
- **Supplier search is NAICS + state only.** It cannot filter by contracting office, installation or PSC.
- **Small-business status is the firm's own SAM representation**, not an SBA size determination.
- **No PSC** was supplied, so §5 PSC is Unknown.
- **Pricing:** GSA CALC has no labor rates for 236220; nothing is estimated. The IGE is the KO's.
- **Procurement method and number of offers** are not reported by USASpending for these awards (shown as —).
- **Agency set-aside shares** (FY2025) are a floor on small-business participation and are deliberately not compared with the 23% goal (different denominator).
