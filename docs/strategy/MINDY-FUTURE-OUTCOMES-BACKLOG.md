# Mindy — Future Customer Outcomes Backlog

**Status:** Product ideas for future development; **not** an authorization to build, merge, deploy, or change pricing.
**Updated:** 2026-11-17
**Source:** Our GovCon Roadmap discussion, OpenGov customer experience, and research-led reorganization of beginner GovCon needs.
**Roadmap reference:** GovCon Giants Roadmap — Set Up, Get Known, Bid, Grow, Deliver (27 steps). The source link is kept privately.

## Product principles

- **People buy outcomes, not features.** Use everyday language about what customers want done.
- **The GovCon Giants Roadmap is the blueprint:** Set Up, Get Known, Bid, Grow, Deliver (27 steps). This backlog is a set of possible outputs, **not** 50 separate applications or a replacement roadmap.
- **Ask Mindy is the execution interface.** The Opportunity Map drives discovery and should ultimately be controllable conversationally. Users can browse without being forced into a workflow.
- **Capability statement generation is a proven acquisition use case** from the user's earlier OpenGov business. SAM.gov preparation and small-business certification readiness are other gateway needs. Contracts attract curiosity, but realistic buyer development and delivery sustain value.
- **Bring information → add federal intelligence → produce a useful result.** Support conversational upload, analysis, grounded document creation, saved references, and later revisions.
- **Vault storage is private by default.** Permitted uploaded and generated documents belong to the authorized account/workspace; reference material does not automatically become verified company fact. Do not require manual Vault setup to begin.
- **No classified information. No CUI unless explicitly authorized under verified controls.** Never claim FedRAMP authorization without evidence.
- **Official actions remain external** unless a supported, authorized integration exists. Mindy prepares materials and guides users; SBA decides certification, SAM.gov handles registration, buyers decide awards.
- **Free navigation; paid intelligence work under approved credit policy.** Do not invent new billing rules for this backlog.

## Proposed priority: acquisition and retention

1. **Get registered and ready:** concrete early wins, strongest initial acquisition candidates.
2. **Find contracts:** high-curiosity entry point and Opportunity Map usage.
3. **Find government customers:** turns browsing into real business development.
4. **Prepare to compete:** higher-intent research and bid decisions.
5. **Run and grow:** ongoing operational and retention needs.

These are **editorial product priorities**, not measured demand shares. Validate against actual user behavior and current data before shipping or promising outcomes.

## What not to lead with

Business Plan; Federal Contracting Business Model; Business Growth Roadmap; Custom Business Intelligence Report; Executive Market Intelligence Briefing. These may remain available on request but are weaker beginner-facing promises than registration, certification, capability statements, contracts, and buyers.
