The purpose of this EOI is to identify interested and qualified vendors for future procurement opportunities involving inspection, cleaning, and maintenance of commercial HVAC duct systems. BACKGROUND INL operates a diverse portfolio of office, laboratory, industrial, and support facilities throughout Idaho. To maintain healthy indoor air quality, support HVAC system performance, and comply with applicable operational and safety requirements, periodic cleaning of air distribution systems may be required. BEA is conducting market research to identify contractors with demonstrated experience performing commercial duct cleaning services in occupied and industrial environments while maintaining compliance with applicable safety, environmental, and quality requirements.
