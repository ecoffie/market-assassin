The scope of work involves renovation for nurse call system in the main hospital and CLC at the VA Cincinnati Site. Work includes, but is not limited to, general construction, alterations, electrical, and telecommunications necessary for demolition and removal of existing nurse call completion and installation of new work. A full list of requirements and further details are provided in the project drawings and specifications. The above construction Statement of Work (SOW) constitutes a basic outline of work and in no way outlines all details for the construction of this project. It is recommended that a detailed inspection of the premises, drawings and specifications be initiated to determine the needs and conditions for the construction of this project.
