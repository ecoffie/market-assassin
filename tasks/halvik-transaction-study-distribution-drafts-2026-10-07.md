## 1. LinkedIn — GovCon Giants page

> **What does the public record show about a $210 million acquisition?**
>
> On January 22, 2026, Tetra Tech announced it had acquired Halvik, a federal IT and data-analytics contractor. Tetra Tech's filings put the fair value of the purchase price at approximately $210 million.
>
> The Mindy Institute reconstructed Halvik's federal prime-contract record as it stood the day before the announcement:
>
> → **245** prime awards and contract vehicles
> → **$724.7M** in cumulative public federal obligations since FY2014
> → **$3.1M** of obligations in FY2017, **$186.7M** in FY2025
> → **92.6%** of obligations came through small-business, 8(a) or women-owned set-aside awards
> → **4 departments** (Defense, Transportation, Commerce, NASA) account for 94.1%
>
> We also checked Halvik's public claims against the record. All 17 vehicles it named are real and held by Halvik. NASA's largest award ties out within 0.03%. Three dollar claims can't be tied to any specific contract.
>
> What the record can't tell you matters just as much: revenue, backlog, margins and why the buyer paid what it paid.
>
> Full study, method and sources: getmindy.ai/research/halvik-tetra-tech

>

## 5. Podcast discussion framing

**Segment title:** "Reading an acquisition from the public record"

Prompts for the conversation:
- **For guests who have sold or bought a GovCon firm:** "When a buyer looked at your contract list, what did they ask first? Was it concentration, recompetes, set-asides?"
- **Vehicles vs. contracts:** Halvik's five largest vehicles carried 78% of obligations. Is the vehicle the asset, or the relationship behind it?
- **What the public record misses:** revenue recognition, subcontract work, CPARS and pipeline. Which of these mattered most in a deal you've seen?
- **Set-asides and ownership change:** keep it to "this is the exposure a diligence team flags". No predictions about eligibility. Point listeners to counsel and the regulation, not to us.

**Guardrail for hosts:** no claims about Halvik's 8(a) dates, revenue, or what happens to its contracts.
